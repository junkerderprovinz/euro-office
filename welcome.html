Euro Office is running

Euro Office is the editor behind OpenCloud, and this page is all it shows on its own. You open and create documents in OpenCloud, and they open in Euro Office right there.

Connect OpenCloud

On the Unraid Docker tab, click the OpenCloud icon and choose Edit. Set these three fields and click Apply:

Web office suite
euro-office
Office document server URL
http://YOUR-SERVER-IP:9900
Office WOPI secret
exactly the same text as the JWT secret of this Euro Office container

After that, the New button in OpenCloud offers documents, spreadsheets and presentations. OpenCloud shows the editor under its own address, so there is no certificate to accept. This needs the OpenCloud container from version 1.4.0 on.

Nextcloud or ownCloud

They load the editor straight from Euro Office, so give them the https address (port 9943 unless you mapped another one) and accept the browser's certificate warning once on every device.
