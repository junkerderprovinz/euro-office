Euro Office is running

Euro Office is the editor behind OpenCloud, and this page is all it shows on its own. You open and create documents in OpenCloud, and they open in Euro Office right there.

Connect OpenCloud

On the Unraid Docker tab, click the OpenCloud icon, choose Edit and switch to Advanced View (top right). Set these three fields and click Apply:

Web office suite
euro-office
Office document server URL
https://YOUR-SERVER-IP:9943
Office WOPI secret
exactly the same text as the JWT secret of this Euro Office container

After that, the New button in OpenCloud offers documents, spreadsheets and presentations.

Certificate

Euro Office uses a certificate it created for itself. Open this page once over https and accept the browser's warning, otherwise the browser will not show the editor inside OpenCloud.
